ABOUT FIZZ N' FLUFF

A little soda stand with a big personality.

Fizz N' Fluff is a locally owned dirty soda pop-up serving handcrafted specialty sodas, creamy cold foam, and customizable drinks throughout the Mississippi Delta.

We are starting with pop-ups so we can bring colorful drinks, friendly service, and plenty of fun to community gatherings and local events.
