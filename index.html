ABOUT FIZZ & FLUFF

A little soda stand with a big personality.

Fizz & Fluff is a new Mississippi Delta dirty soda business built around creative drinks, friendly service, and plenty of fun. We are starting as a pop-up so we can bring specialty sodas to community events, festivals, and local gatherings.

Our goal is simple: serve colorful, affordable drinks that make people smile—one fizzy sip at a time.
